# How to play Metro Dash

## Objective

Run as far as you can across the metro rooftops. Tickets, close calls and missions all feed your score — and three missions per run (shown as chips at the top) pay a +150 bonus each when cleared. Your best distance is saved on this device.

## The HUD

- **Distance** — meters covered. Speed climbs the further you go.
- **Tickets** — collected transit tickets, +10 each.
- **Speed** — your current pace in km/h.
- **Mission chips** — this run's three objectives with live progress.

## The moves

| Situation | Do this |
| --- | --- |
| Crates, roof gaps | **Jump** — early for gaps, the crate is forgiving |
| Signal gantry (full width, head height) | **Slide** — jumping into one is a crash |
| Hovering drone | **Change lanes** — or slide under it for a near-miss |
| Oncoming train | Watch for the flashing **!** on its lane, then be somewhere else |
| Boost pad | Ride it — 2 seconds of speed + you smash through obstacles |
| Ticket cluster | Detour for it — missions often demand a stack |

## Near-misses

Passing an obstacle within a coat-hem triggers **CLOSE! +25** with a brief slow-motion beat. Chaining close calls is a mission type — but greed is how runs end. A near-miss you survive pays; a near-miss you misjudge doesn't.

## Missions

Three per run, drawn from a pool: collect N tickets, N close calls, reach N meters. Each cleared mission pays +150 and Ziggy reacts. The results screen shows how many you cleared — three for the perfect commute.

## Crash and the debrief

Hitting an obstacle at speed triggers the crash cam and ends the run. Before the results board appears, the **rooftop debrief** poses one question from the scam-radar curriculum — spotting the real bank domain, reading an HTTP 503, why urgency is a weapon. Correct answers are worth +150 on the final board; wrong ones get a one-line explanation. There is no penalty for skipping.

## Strategy quick-reference

1. The route generates fair patterns — there is always a workable lane, jump or slide. Read two obstacles ahead, not one.
2. Tickets on the far lane are rarely worth a lane-switch into an unseen drone. Check, then commit.
3. Speed is the real enemy: at high pace, react to gantries on sight and pre-empt drones by lane, not by dodge.
4. Boost pads make you invincible for two seconds — a free ticket lane even through a drone line.
